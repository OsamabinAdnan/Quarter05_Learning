**Art style:**

Blend **Leonardo da Vinci–inspired sketch aesthetics** with modern infographic design:

- Fine ink linework, anatomical sketch style (like Renaissance notebooks)
- Light cross-hatching and hand-drawn diagrams (muscle fibers, human figure, weights)
- Subtle parchment or aged paper texture background
- Handwritten-style headings paired with clean, highly legible body text

**Content structure (keep text concise and easy to read):**

1. **Progressive Overload** – gradually increase weight or reps
2. **Nutrition** – high protein intake, caloric surplus
3. **Training Frequency** – hit each muscle group 2x per week
4. **Recovery** – sleep, rest days, muscle repair
5. **Consistency** – long-term adherence over perfection
(Optional 6th block: **Form & Technique**)

**Typography:**

- Section titles: elegant, slightly handwritten or serif style
- Body text: simple, modern, highly readable
- Clear hierarchy and spacing—prioritize clarity over decoration

**Visual elements:**

- Small Da Vinci–style anatomical sketches (muscle diagrams, proportions)
- Minimal icons integrated into each section
- Thin divider lines and subtle geometric guides (like notebook margins)

A fusion of **Renaissance scientific sketchbook** and **modern productivity card**—intellectually beautiful, calm, and highly readable. Avoid clutter. Prioritize clarity, balance, and taste.

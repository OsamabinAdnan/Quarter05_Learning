Create a cozy, vintage-style recipe infographic poster for “Grandma’s Famous Chocolate Chip Cookies.”

The design should feel warm, nostalgic, and slightly rustic, like an old family recipe passed down for generations. Use a soft cream or lightly textured paper background.

At the top, include a large, hand-lettered title:

“Grandma’s Famous Chocolate Chip Cookies” with playful handwritten accents like hearts or underlines.

Include a short, handwritten-style description underneath, something warm and inviting about crispy edges, soft centers, and nostalgia.

**Layout: split into two main sections:**

**LEFT SIDE (Ingredients):**

- Display ingredients as realistic, top-down food photography in small bowls or neatly arranged (flour, butter cubes, brown sugar, white sugar, eggs, vanilla, baking soda, salt, chocolate chips).
- Each ingredient should have a handwritten label and quantity next to it.
- Add small doodles or arrows pointing to ingredients.

**RIGHT SIDE (Instructions):**

- Step-by-step instructions (6–8 steps), written in a casual handwritten font.
- Include small sketched icons next to steps (whisk, oven, mixing bowl, cookie scoop, etc.).
- Add little handwritten notes like:
    - “Don’t overmix!”
    - “Try not to eat all the chocolate chips first :)”
    - “Grandma’s secret: slightly underbake”

**Hero Visual:**

- Include a large, semi-realistic photo of freshly baked chocolate chip cookies on a plate (golden brown, gooey chocolate).
- Optionally add a small sticky note with a handwritten tip.

**Extra details:**

- Add arrows, callouts, and soft pastel highlights.
- Include a small polaroid-style image of cookies cooling on a rack.
- Finish with a cozy footer note like:
“The best cookies are made to be shared. Enjoy with a cold glass of milk.”

**Style notes:**

- Semi-realistic food photography mixed with hand-drawn elements
- Soft shadows, warm lighting
- Imperfect, natural handwriting (not too clean or digital)
- Slightly scrapbook / editorial infographic feel

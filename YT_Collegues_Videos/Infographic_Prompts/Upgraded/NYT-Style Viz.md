**Create a clean, high-end editorial infographic centered around a single data chart showing the rise of microplastics over time.**

**Style & Layout:**

- Minimalist, publication-quality design (think: serious, analytical, modern)
- Neutral color palette: off-white background, black/charcoal text, muted accent color (e.g. deep blue or rust)
- Elegant typography: serif headline, sans-serif body text
- Plenty of whitespace, strong grid alignment

**Main Chart (centerpiece):**

- Line chart showing **microplastics in the environment over time (1950–2050 projection)**
- X-axis: Year (1950 → present → future projection)
- Y-axis: Microplastic concentration / volume (relative or indexed)
- Clear upward exponential curve

**Data to reflect:**

- Plastic production grew from ~2 million tons in 1950 to 450+ million today
- Microplastic exposure/ingestion has increased ~6x since 1990
- ~2.7 million tonnes of microplastics entered environment in 2020, projected to double by 2040
- Ocean microplastics accumulation has risen significantly over decades alongside plastic production

**Annotations (very important):**

- Use thin lines and arrows pointing to key inflection points:
    - “Mass plastic production begins (1950s)”
    - “Acceleration in global plastic use (1980–2000)”
    - “Microplastics detected in human body (2000s–2020s)”
- Small, precise labels—not cluttered

**Pullout Quotes / Callouts:**

- Include 2–3 short, bold editorial-style callouts:
    - “Microplastic consumption has increased sixfold since 1990”
    - “Microplastics are now found in air, water, and human organs”
    - “Projected to double by 2040 if trends continue”

**Supporting Micro-Visuals (subtle):**

- Small icons or mini diagrams:
    - Plastic bottle → fragments → microplastics
    - Human silhouette with particles
    - Ocean particles

**Tone:**

- Analytical, slightly alarming but not sensational
- Designed to feel like a data-driven insight piece

**Output:**

- High-resolution vertical infographic
- Balanced between scientific clarity and editorial storytelling

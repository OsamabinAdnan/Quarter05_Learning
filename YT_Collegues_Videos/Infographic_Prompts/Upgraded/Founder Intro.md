Create a high-quality, cinematic 3D animated character portrait of a startup founder, designed in a warm, expressive, stylized animation style (soft lighting, rounded features, slightly exaggerated expressions, highly polished rendering).

**Core Concept:**

This is not just a profile — it’s a *mini origin story*. The viewer should immediately feel curious and slightly surprised by the founder’s journey.

---

**Scene & Composition:**

- The character stands centered in a clean, softly lit environment (subtle gradient or minimal studio backdrop).
- Add faint environmental hints tied to their story (e.g., a small object, texture, or silhouette in the background that nods to their past).
- Shallow depth of field for a cinematic look.
- Soft global illumination with a gentle rim light.

---

**Character Design:**

- Stylized, expressive human with slightly larger eyes and smooth features.
- Outfit reflects a founder who didn’t take a traditional path (e.g., slightly unconventional but tasteful—vintage jacket, worn-in sneakers, unique accessory).
- Expression: confident, but with warmth and a hint of playfulness.

**Story-Driven Callouts (IMPORTANT):**

Surround the character with 5–7 floating callouts that feel like *reveals*, not bullet points.

Each callout should be short, punchy, and slightly unexpected.

**Use this exact sample narrative (or very close to it):**

- “Built first app at 14… it failed in 3 days”
- “Lived out of a car for 6 months chasing this idea”
- “Turned $2k into a $3.2M ARR company”
- “Got rejected by 27 investors”
- “Now helping 50,000+ people work smarter”
- “Still designs everything at 2am”
- “Secret obsession: collecting vintage keyboards”

---

**Callout Design:**

- Floating around the character in a balanced composition
- Connected with thin, elegant lines or soft arrows
- Rounded cards with subtle glassmorphism (semi-transparent, blurred background)
- Soft pastel palette (muted blues, purples, greens with warm accents)
- Slight glow to make them feel alive

**Typography:**

- Clean, modern sans-serif
- Mix of regular and bold for emphasis on key phrases (numbers, surprising words)

---

**Rendering Quality:**

- Ultra-detailed, high resolution
- Soft shadows, realistic materials
- Premium, studio-level finish
- No noise, no clutter

---

**Mood & Tone:**

- Inspiring but grounded
- Feels like discovering a hidden backstory
- “I didn’t expect that” energy
- Polished, modern, high taste

---

**Camera:**

- Slight 3/4 angle
- Framed waist-up
- Subtle cinematic perspective (not flat)

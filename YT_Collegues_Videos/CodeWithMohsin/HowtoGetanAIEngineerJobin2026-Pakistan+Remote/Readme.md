# Practical Guide: Landing an AI Engineer Job in Pakistan & Remotely

## 1. Focus on Practical Skills Over Certifications

- **Certifications carry little weight** with employers.
- Companies focus heavily on what you can build1. Key expected capabilities include:
  - Integrating AI models into production software.
  - Working with APIs and building **LLM applications**.
  - Understanding **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**, **AI Agents**, and **workflow automation**.
  - Demonstrating clean, functional code on **GitHub**.

---

## 2. Use Diverse Search Keywords

Searching solely for "AI Engineer" leads to missed opportunities because Pakistani and global companies use varied job titles for similar skill sets23. Search using these alternate titles:

- **Core AI Roles:** `AI Engineer`, `AI Developer`, `Generative AI Engineer`, `LLM Engineer`, `LLM Application Developer`.
- **Automation & Agents:** `Agentic AI Developer`, `AI Agent Developer`, `AI Automation Engineer`, `AI Solution Engineer`.
- **Junior & Specialized Roles:** `Junior AI Engineer`, `Junior AI Developer`, `Python AI Developer`, `Full Stack AI Engineer`, `AI/ML Engineer`, `Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)`.

---

## 3. Top Job Platforms to Target

Set up complete profiles across all major platforms:

- **LinkedIn:** Filter searches by location (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad), set the time filter to **Past 24 Hours**, and search for internships or junior positions.
- **Wellfound (AngelList):** The best platform for discovering early-stage AI startups hiring for open positions (great for fresh grads and students).
- **Pakistani Portals:** Use **Indeed Pakistan** (`pk.indeed.com`), **Mustakbil** (`mustakbil.com`), and **Rozee.pk** for local openings and internships.
- **Upwork:** A strong option for remote freelance contracts and high-paying gig work in AI development and automation.

---

## 4. Application & Outreach Tactics

- **Direct Outreach:** Connect directly with **Founders and CTOs** on LinkedIn or via email rather than relying only on standard application portals.
- **Apply Broadly:** Even if you don't meet 100% of the listed job requirements, submit your application if you have core AI engineering capabilities.

---

## 5. Daily Workflow

- **Commit 1 Hour Daily:** Spend at least one hour every day across these 5–6 platforms searching for roles, sending customized applications, and messaging hiring leads.
- **Build Your Artifacts:** Prioritize building a public **GitHub repository** and **project portfolio** to prove your technical ability.

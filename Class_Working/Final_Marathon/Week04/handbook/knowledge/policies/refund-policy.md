## Eligibility

Customers can return an item within **30 days** of purchase with a valid receipt. Items must be unused and in their original condition.

## Faulty Goods Exception

Faulty or defective goods are exempt from the 30-day window and may be returned at any time, regardless of condition.

## Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed and land in the customer's account within **5 working days** of approval.

## Requirements

To qualify for a refund, the following must be provided:

- Original purchase receipt
- Item in unused condition (except for faulty goods)
- Return initiated within 30 days of purchase (except for faulty goods)

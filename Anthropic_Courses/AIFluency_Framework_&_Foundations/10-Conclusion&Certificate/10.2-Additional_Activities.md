# Lesson 10.2: Additional Activities for Self-Directed Practice

---

> [!TIP]
> **Self-Directed Learning:** The following activities are designed for self-directed exploration after completing the *AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations* course. Choose the ones that resonate with your professional interests and goals—or use them as inspiration to design your own!

---

## 1. Activity 01: Build a Personal AI Fluency Plan

Collaborate with an AI assistant (like Claude) as an active thinking partner to design a structured, realistic roadmap for developing your AI fluency over time.

### Step 1: Current Competency Assessment
* **Audit the 4Ds**: Rate your current proficiency (*Novice*, *Developing*, *Confident*) across **Delegation**, **Description**, **Discernment**, and **Diligence**.
* **Identify Strengths & Gaps**: Document specific proficiencies and blind spots within each competency.
* **Evaluate Interaction Modes**: Identify which interaction modes (**Automation**, **Augmentation**, or **Agency**) you currently leverage most—and which you underutilize.

### Step 2: Development Priority Identification
* **Select 1–2 Focus Areas**: Pick one or two core competencies to intentionally build first.
* **Contextual Value**: Identify specific sub-skills (e.g., *Process Description* or *Deployment Diligence*) that offer the highest leverage in your daily work.
* **Target Interaction Mode**: Determine which interaction mode you want to stretch into (e.g., transitioning from simple automation to augmentative thinking partnership).

### Step 3: Actionable Execution Plan
* **Design Targeted Drills**: Create specific exercises and everyday work tasks to practice each chosen competency.
* **Cadence & Milestones**: Establish a realistic schedule for deliberate practice sessions.
* **Success Metrics**: Define tangible indicators that demonstrate measurable progress and mastery.

---

## 2. Activity 02: Build a Personal Prompt & Pattern Library

Curate a living, reusable repository of high-performing prompts, context templates, and reasoning patterns tailored to your recurring tasks.

### Step 1: Common Task Template Creation
* **Identify Core Workflows**: List 5–10 tasks you perform repeatedly with AI (e.g., meeting synthesis, code review, outline generation, draft editing).
* **Develop Reusable Templates**: Draft structured prompt templates that consistently yield high-quality outputs.
* **Insert Dynamic Placeholders**: Use clear brackets (e.g., `[Insert Audience]`, `[Insert Constraints]`) for variable data added during execution.

### Step 2: Strategy & Reasoning Documentation
* **Document Description Techniques**: Record specific phrasing, personas, and framing strategies that work best for specific outputs.
* **Catalog Discernment Checklists**: Keep a checklist of recurring errors, biases, or format lapses to probe for during evaluation.
* **Map to Interaction Modes**: Note which prompt structures suit rapid automation versus deep collaborative augmentation.

### Step 3: Organization & Maintenance
* **Centralized Accessibility**: Store your library in a searchable format (e.g., Markdown notes, Notion, or snippet manager).
* **Include Gold-Standard Examples**: Pair templates with examples of successful outputs for reference.
* **Continuous Refinement**: Regularly update and prune templates as models evolve.

## 3. Activity 03: Game Night! Description & Discernment Workouts

Puzzles and games have explicit rules, tight constraints, and objective solutions—making them ideal sandboxes to hone precision **Description** and sharpen critical **Discernment** without the pressure of live business deliverables.

### Game 1: Swap Riddles
* **The Exercise**: Create or select a clever riddle and ask Claude to guess the answer.
  > *Example Riddle*: "Five-score and more drums I tap to paint a symphony of light. What am I doing?"  
  > *Answer*: Typing on a computer keyboard (~104 keys) to create pixels on a monitor.
* **The Process**: Have Claude explain its step-by-step reasoning. Do not reveal the answer immediately; gently guide Claude toward the right chain of thought using subtle hints and context.
* **Switch Sides**: Have Claude create a riddle for you and guide your reasoning in return.

### Game 2: Co-operative Crosswords
* **The Exercise**: Solve challenging or cryptic crossword clues together.
* **The Focus**: Practice real-time steering and iterative refinement:
  * *"Remember, it must be exactly 5 letters."*
  * *"It can't be that because the third letter is 'R'."*
  * *"Consider a double meaning: could 'swallow' refer to the bird rather than the action?"*
* **Skill Transfer**: These conversational steering skills directly transfer to guiding complex project tasks.

### Game 3: Word Association & Pattern Recognition
* Select 12–20 random words and collaborate with Claude to identify non-obvious thematic links, conceptual groupings, or hidden relationships.
* Guide Claude when it falls into rigid interpretations, and stay open to connections you might have overlooked.

| Discernment Lens | Diagnostic Practice in Games |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Product Discernment** | Do the proposed answers make logical sense and genuinely satisfy the puzzle's constraints? |
| **Process Discernment** | Did Claude explore multiple hypotheses, or did it suffer from tunnel vision on a single interpretation? |
| **Performance Discernment** | How effectively did Claude communicate its reasoning? Did it build productively on your suggestions? |

## 4. More Playful Challenge Ideas

| Game / Challenge | Core Skill Developed | Practice Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Twenty Questions** | Constraint-based inquiry | Take turns guessing a person, concept, or object using only yes/no questions. |
| **Collaborative Storytelling** | Context retention & coherence | Alternate sentences or paragraphs back and forth while maintaining narrative tension and character consistency. |
| **Tavern Wizard RPG** | Open-ended collaborative roleplay | Engage in interactive text-based role-playing with Claude acting as either player or Game Master. |
| **Concepts & Constraints** | Expressive Description | Explain an advanced technical concept under strict stylistic rules (e.g., *"Explain quantum computing using only culinary metaphors"*). |
| **Advanced Puzzling & Code Challenges** | Complex chain-of-thought analysis | Tackle challenging mathematical or logic problems (e.g., [Project Euler](https://projecteuler.net/)) collaboratively. |
